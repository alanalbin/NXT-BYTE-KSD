Why we exist

Mission & Vision

Two halves of the same idea: what we do every week, and where it is all pointed.

01

Our Mission

To make entrepreneurship a hands-on habit rather than a lecture topic. We give every student a room to test ideas in, mentors who have shipped before, and the momentum to go from a rough sketch to a working prototype in weeks — not semesters.

  • Run practical build sprints, hackathons and founder sessions
  • Connect students with mentors, alumni and early-stage investors
  • Turn campus problems into fundable, buildable products
02

Our Vision

A campus where starting something is the default, not the exception — where the next generation of engineers graduates having already built, broken and rebuilt something real, and where a student idea can find its first users before it finds a grade.

  • A self-sustaining founder community that outlives each batch
  • Student-led ventures with real traction beyond the campus gate
  • An innovation culture measured in launches, not certificates
